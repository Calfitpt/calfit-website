How We Help

Specialized Care for Your Lifestyle

We combine hands-on manual therapy with targeted exercise programming to get you moving effortlessly.

Pain & Orthopedic Care

Help for back and neck pain, shoulder and knee problems, joint stiffness, and common muscle or joint injuries. We’ll work with you to improve movement, build strength, and return to daily activities.

Vestibular & Balance Therapy

Individualized rehabilitation for appropriate dizziness, vertigo, and balance concerns, with the goal of improving stability and confidence in everyday movement.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation after eligible orthopedic procedures, tailored to your surgeon’s protocol, your stage of recovery, and your functional goals.

Sports Rehabilitation & Performance

Build strength, mobility, and movement confidence as you recover from injury and work toward returning to running, training, recreation, or sport.

Conditions We Commonly Treat

  • Arthritis
  • Joint replacement
  • Vertigo disorder
  • Cardiovascular disease, neuritis and stroke
  • Chronic fatigue and fibromyalgia
  • Ankle strain, foot strain and plantar fasciitis
  • Tennis elbow and carpal tunnel syndrome
  • ACL reconstruction
  • Muscle sprains and strain
  • Shoulder and arm pain
  • Spinal conditions
  • Auto and personal injuries
  • Work-related injuries
  • Pre- and post-orthopedic surgery

Meet The Team

CalFit is proudly owned and operated locally, dedicated to elevating the standard of care in South San Jose.

Physical Therapist & Co-Director

Neha Joshi, PT

Neha completed her Bachelor of Physiotherapy, followed by a Master of Physiotherapy in Musculoskeletal Sciences in 2014 from India. She has been practicing physical therapy in various outpatient settings in San Jose, CA, since 2021 and specializes in complex orthopedic injuries and post partum corrective exercises. She believes in an active approach to rehab, empowering her patients with the tools they need for recovery and return to activities they love.

Outside of work, she loves to dance and perform, and has been learning Kathak (an Indian classical dance form). She also loves traveling and spending time her family and friends.

Physical Therapist & Co-Director

Poorva, PT

Bringing 12 years of extensive clinical experience to CalFit, Poorva specializes in delivering highly personalized, evidence-based physical therapy. She is dedicated to helping patients overcome acute and chronic pain, restore function, and achieve their long-term health and mobility goals.

Options & Pricing

Payment & Insurance

We're currently completing insurance credentialing. While that process is underway, CalFit Physical Therapy offers self-pay appointments so you can begin working toward your movement and recovery goals.

Self-pay appointments

We believe you should understand the cost of care before your visit. Contact us for our current evaluation and follow-up visit rates.

Using out-of-network benefits

If your health plan includes out-of-network physical therapy benefits, we can provide a superbill upon request. Reimbursement depends on your plan, and payment is not guaranteed.

Questions about coverage or cost?

Contact us before booking, and we'll help you understand your available options.
